04 Network activity

Baby Mode makes no network requests. Every playground, every setting and the stats screen work identically with the phone in Aeroplane Mode, and we encourage you to check.

The only exception is Apple’s own StoreKit, the system framework that handles the subscription. When you start a free trial, subscribe, or tap Restore Purchases, iOS contacts Apple — not us — to complete or verify that transaction. That traffic is between you and Apple.

05 The subscription, and Apple’s role

Baby Mode is free to download and includes a seven-day free trial, after which it requires an auto-renewing subscription. All payment is processed by Apple through the App Store.

Your Apple Account, your card details and your billing address are entered into a system screen drawn by iOS, outside our app. Our code cannot read that screen and never receives your payment details, your name, your email address or your billing address.

The app asks iOS a single question on device — is this person subscribed — and shows or hides the paywall accordingly. We do not run a server, we do not validate receipts on a server, and we do not keep a subscriber list. Apple is the merchant of record; that transaction is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy and the Apple Media Services Terms and Conditions.

Apple also gives us aggregate, anonymised sales and download reports — how many units, in which countries, on which days. Those reports are produced by Apple, contain no personal information, and cannot be traced to an individual.

06 Children’s privacy — COPPA

Children under 13

Baby Mode collects no personal information from anyone, and that includes children. Not their name, not their age, not their voice, not their picture, not their location, and no identifier that could be used to recognise them later or across other apps.

Baby Mode is designed to be handed to a baby or toddler by an adult. We have therefore built it on the assumption that a child under 13 will use it, and we have designed it so that the question of collecting a child’s data never arises.

Under the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA, 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506) and the FTC’s COPPA Rule (16 C.F.R. Part 312):

  • We do not collect, use or disclose personal information from children under 13, so no verifiable parental consent is required and none is sought.
  • We do not condition a child’s participation on disclosing more information than is reasonably necessary — we ask for none at all.
  • We do not use persistent identifiers for behavioural advertising, audience measurement, profiling or any purpose beyond supporting internal operations, because we do not use persistent identifiers.
  • There is no advertising in the app, of any kind, to anyone.
  • There are no third parties in the app. No analytics provider, no ad network, no software development kit written by anyone but Apple. No one else receives anything, because no one else is there.
  • The app contains no chat, no messaging, no user profiles, no social features, no sharing, no photo or video upload, and no way for a child to publish or transmit anything.
  • The app contains no links out to the open web and no advertising links. The only external links are on the adult-facing screens: this policy, the Terms of Use, and our support page.
  • Because we hold no children’s personal information, there is nothing to retain and nothing to delete. A parent who wishes to remove everything the app has stored can delete the app.

If you are a parent or guardian and you believe a child has somehow provided us with personal information, contact us at granttrudeau@gmail.com. We will investigate and delete anything found. We expect this email never to be necessary.

Baby Mode is not listed in the App Store’s Kids Category. That is a distribution choice, not a privacy one. The protections described in this section apply regardless of which App Store category the app appears in.

09 Tracking, advertising and Do Not Track

Baby Mode does not track you across apps or websites, does not build a profile, and does not link anything to your identity. It never shows Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it has no reason to ask.

Because we do no tracking, Do Not Track browser signals and Global Privacy Control signals have nothing to disable. We honour them by default and by construction.

10 The website

This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no tracking pixels, no social widgets and no third-party scripts. The only external request any page makes is to Google Fonts, to load the two typefaces this page is set in; that request tells Google your IP address and browser, as any request for a file does. If that matters to you, block fonts.googleapis.com and the pages will fall back to Helvetica and render perfectly well.

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, which may include IP addresses, for security and abuse prevention. We do not use those logs to identify anyone and do not combine them with anything else.

11 Security

The strongest security property of Baby Mode is that there is nothing to secure. There is no database of ours, no cloud account, no API key, no backup and no export. Data that never leaves a device cannot be breached at rest on a server, intercepted in transit, or leaked by a vendor.

What is on the phone is protected by iOS: app sandboxing, file protection tied to your passcode, and Face ID or Touch ID if you use them. Keeping your phone updated and passcode-locked is the whole of the security advice we have.
